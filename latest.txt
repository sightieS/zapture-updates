notes = Zapture 0.6.1 · New · Clip sound: a short three-note chime plays when a clip is saved, so you know it worked even in a fullscreen game. Turn it on or off in Settings → Clipping → "Sound when a clip is saved", and press Play to hear it. · Shortcuts without Ctrl, Alt or Win: global shortcuts can now be any key, including a plain letter like G or Shift+G. If you pick a key you type with, Zapture explains that pressing it anywhere will run the action instead of typing. Keys Windows reserves are still blocked. · Website links: Settings → About now links to the Korven website, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, End User Licence Agreement (new), Contact & support, Report a bug and Feature requests. · Fixes · Links now open the exact section of a page (for example, the FAQ on the support page). · The FFmpeg licence note in Settings → About now says correctly that Zapture downloads FFmpeg for you.
