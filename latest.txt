notes = Zapture 0.7.1 · New · A better tray menu. It shows your own shortcuts. While recording, Stop and Pause come first. You can take a screenshot of an area, a window or a whole monitor, save a clip, switch Clipping on or off, and open your Screenshots, Recordings or Clips folder, all from the tray. It's dark when Zapture's theme is dark. · Copy a colour from the screen. While selecting, press Ctrl+H to copy the HEX code of the pixel under the pointer, for example 1A2B3C. You can change the key in Settings → Shortcuts. · Move the editing bar. Drag it by its grip to anywhere on your screen. Double-click the grip to put it back. · Improved · The editing bar gets out of the way. It fades while you draw, move something or type underneath it, so you can see and reach what's under it. Point at it to bring it back. · Only one Zapture runs at a time. Opening Zapture again brings the one that's already running to the front. · Fixed · When zoomed in, the editing bar could be placed as if the screen were larger than it is.
