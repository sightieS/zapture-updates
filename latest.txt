notes = Zapture 1.2.3 · Fixed · - **Closing Zapture could leave a black box on the screen.** · - Windows animates a window closing from a snapshot of it. For Zapture's own-drawn, see-through windows, that snapshot could come out black and stay on screen. · - Zapture's windows now disappear at once, with no animation: the main window when it goes to the tray, and notifications and pinned pictures when they close. · - When Zapture quits, every window it has disappears at once. It used to put the main window back on screen for a moment first, undrawn. · - **The pen and highlighter make a dot when you click once**, in the region editor and the image editor. It's as wide as the pen and saved in the picture; Ctrl+Z removes it. · - **Text you're typing no longer wraps onto a new line.** · - The text box starts wider, grows ahead of what you type, and keeps each line on one line, as it will be saved. · - Use Shift+Enter for a new line, and Enter to finish.
