notes = Zapture 0.7.0 · Fixed · - **Exports with a target size no longer fail** with "Error reading log file … for pass-2 encoding". Newer FFmpeg versions applied two-pass settings to the audio as well, which can't use them. Two-pass now applies to video only, and every export has its own log file. · - **Clips save in under a second and end exactly when you press the key.** Sound stays in sync with the picture. · - **No more FFmpeg left running in the background** after Zapture closes, even if it closes unexpectedly. · - **The video preview no longer freezes after you resume from pause.** · - **The editor toolbar stays on one screen** when your selection is at the edge of a monitor. · - **Opening the region selector is smoother**, with no flash or jump. The screen dims in gently. · New · - **Copy text**: pull the words out of any capture and paste them anywhere. Recognition runs on your PC, using Windows' built-in text recognition. · - **Pin to screen**: keep a capture floating above your other windows. Drag it, scroll to zoom, right-click to copy or save, double-click to close. · - **Library search**: find captures by name, and filter by date and format. · - **Better text**: move, resize and edit text after placing it. Choose the font, size, bold, colour, alignment, a background box or an outline. · - **Zoom in the editor**: Ctrl+scroll or Ctrl + / − / 0, and drag with Space or the middle button to move around. · - **Any colour**: type a HEX code. Your recent colours are kept. · - **Blur and pixelate strength**: pick how strong each one is. · - **Style presets**: save the colour, width and text style you use most. · - **Notification time**: choose how long notifications stay (Settings → General). · - **Screenshot delay** of 3, 5 or 10 seconds, for menus and hover states. · - **WebP screenshots** and a JPEG quality setting. · - **More file-name tokens**: `{year}` `{month}` `{day}` `{hour}` `{minute}` `{second}` `{n}`. · - **Copy clips to the clipboard** as soon as they're saved, ready to paste into Discord. · - **Level meters on the recording card**, so you can see that sound is being recorded. · Privacy · Settings → About now lists exactly what Zapture fetches online: update checks and the FFmpeg download. Your captures are never uploaded.
