notes = Zapture 1.1.0 · New · - **What's new after updates.** The first time you open Zapture after an update, a window lists what changed. Open it again any time from Settings → About → What's new. New installs don't see it. · - **Eyedropper in the editor.** Click the eyedropper next to the colours, or press **I**, then click anywhere on the picture. A magnifier shows the pixels and the HEX code under the pointer. Right-click or Esc cancels. It works in the region editor and the image window, and picks from the screenshot itself, not your drawings. · - **Show or hide recent captures** on the Home page: Settings → General → Show recent captures. · Improved · - **Copy text is more accurate.** · - It reads the clean screenshot, so your arrows, shapes and text aren't mixed into the result. Blurred or pixelated parts stay hidden. · - Small screenshots are enlarged smoothly (2–3×) before reading. · - Light text on dark backgrounds (dark mode, terminals, video) is read as well, and merged with the rest. · - Lines come out in reading order. Side-by-side columns are separated by a tab, and paragraphs keep a blank line between them. · - The notification shows a preview of what was copied. · - **A better colour menu in the editor.** It opens just below the bar, or above it near the bottom of the screen, and always stays whole on one monitor. It has the colour picker, the colours, your recent colours, a HEX box and the eyedropper together. Dragging in the picker adds the colour to your recent colours once, when you let go. Clicking outside the menu closes it without drawing. · - **Clipping uses much less performance.** · - The always-on clip buffer now runs at a lower priority than your games. · - It uses your graphics card's fastest low-latency encoder settings: NVIDIA p1/low latency, AMD speed/low latency, Intel veryfast, and CPU ultrafast on 2 threads. · - It uses a cheaper way to resize frames. · - New installs clip at 30 fps. If you still see a drop in games, set Settings → Clips → Frame rate to 30 fps. · - Recordings keep their full-quality settings. · Fixed · - **Win+Shift+S sometimes did nothing** and had to be pressed again. When Windows didn't report that a key was released, Zapture ignored the next press of that key. It now tells a new press apart from a held key by time. · - **The region selector could stay invisible** when an earlier selector was still closing. Each selector window now has its own name, so Zapture always shows the right one, and it keeps making sure the window is visible for its first frames.
