notes = Zapture 1.2.0 · New: open your pictures and videos with Zapture · - **Right-click a picture or video → Edit with Zapture**, or choose Zapture under **Open with**. (On Windows 11, "Edit with Zapture" is under "Show more options".) · - **Pictures open in the editor.** It has arrows, shapes, text, blur and pixelate, crop, turning, copy text and pin. · - **Videos open in the video editor.** You can trim, split and export them. · - **Your originals are never changed.** Saving adds "<name> (edited)" next to the file. · - **Opens fast, without the main window.** · - If Zapture is already running in the tray, the file opens in that copy straight away. No second Zapture starts. · - If Zapture isn't running, it starts reading the picture while its window starts up, and only the editor appears. · - When you close the editor, Zapture quits, unless you keep it running in the tray. · - **Lots of formats.** Pictures are read with Windows' own decoders: PNG, JPEG, HEIC/HEIF, AVIF, WebP, TIFF, GIF, BMP and ICO. HEIC and AVIF need Microsoft's free Store extensions. · - **Phone photos open the right way up.** · - **Videos:** MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, WMV, M4V, MPEG, TS/MTS and 3GP. Edited videos are saved as MP4, MOV or MKV. · - **Make it your default:** Settings → General → Pictures and videos → **Default apps** opens Windows' Default apps page for Zapture. Windows asks you to choose this yourself. Turn **Open with Zapture** off there to remove Zapture from File Explorer. Uninstalling removes it as well. · Fixed · - **The taskbar and Start menu could open over the region selector.** · - While the selector is open, the Windows key and Ctrl+Esc don't reach Windows. · - The selector tells the taskbar it's full-screen, and keeps putting itself back on top of it. · - The Windows key works again as soon as the selector closes. It also comes back by itself within 1.5 seconds if Zapture ever stops. · - **Win+Shift+S could open Snipping Tool while Zapture's own window was in front.** · - The keyboard hook now has a thread of its own, so the tray and menus can never make it answer Windows too late. · - It also keeps its own record of which modifier keys are held, rather than relying only on Windows' report.
