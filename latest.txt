notes = Zapture 1.0.0: the first stable release · Zapture is now stable. Version numbers start again at 1.0.0: the 2.x builds were previews. · **If you have a preview build (2.x) installed**, download and install 1.0.0 once. The preview builds won't offer it automatically, because they compare version numbers. Your settings, shortcuts and editor presets are kept. From 1.0.0 on, updates arrive as usual. · Faster · - **The region selector opens much sooner.** The screen is captured the moment you press the shortcut, while Zapture finds the buttons and panels inside windows at the same time instead of first. The selector also comes on screen a frame earlier, and dims straight away instead of fading in from nothing. · What's in 1.0a · - **Screenshots** of an area, a window, part of a window, a monitor or all monitors, with a magnifier, a delay timer, and **Ctrl+H** to copy the colour under the pointer. · - **An editor** with arrows, shapes, a full text tool, blur and pixelate, any HEX colour, style presets, zoom, undo and redo, and a bar you can move. · - **Copy text** from any capture, and **pin** captures above your windows. · - **Screen recording** up to 4K and 144 fps, with system audio, microphone and live level meters. · - **Instant clips** of the last 15 seconds to 5 minutes, ending exactly when you press the key. · - **A video editor** that trims, splits and exports to MP4, WebM, GIF or MP3, with "Fit under 20 MB". · - **A searchable library**, a full **tray menu**, customisable shortcuts, themes, and only one Zapture running at a time. · - **Private**: no account, no telemetry, and your captures never leave your PC.
